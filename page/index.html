The agent that grows with you

The self-improving AI agent built by Nous Research. A built-in learning loop creates skills from experience, improves them during use, and builds a deepening model of who you are across sessions.

    #4 Style
    
            
    The active skin.
    
            
    
              A skin sets the semantic colours — background, ui_accent,
              ui_tool, ui_ok, ui_warn,
              ui_error — across the whole session. Six ship built in;
              /skin shows or switches the active one, and your own YAML can join them.
            
    
            
    This page wears them too. Press s, or pick one.

        Isolated subagents with their own conversations, terminals and Python RPC scripts, for

      Bot Mode
      
        
      A roster of named bots.
      
        
      
          Named Bots with their own model, memory, skills, routines and chats — bundled and
          default-on in Hermes Desktop.
        
      
        
        
          
      • 
            ResearcherWeb search · vision · MCPIts own memory, so the citations stay with it.
          
        • 
          
      • 
            OpsCron · SSH backend · SlackIts own routines, running unattended through the gateway.
          
        • 
          
      • 
            ReviewerDocker sandbox · worktreesIts own skills, learned on your codebase.
          
        • 
          
      • 
            InboxEmail · Telegram · voice modeIts own chats, and it never mixes them up.
          
        • 
        
      
        
      A roster is yours to name. These four are an example of the shape.
