How it works

Bring the outcome

Tell me what you want the system to do, what you already have, and what must stay under your control.

Agree the boundary

We define the machine, access, data, scope and success condition before touching the setup.

Build and verify

We configure the useful path, test it with representative input, and make failure visible.

Hand it over

You receive the working setup, a short guide and a clear line between included support and future changes.

For people who want control, not theatre.
